# Privacy Policy for SplitYourBill

**Effective Date:** September 20, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 20, 2026  
**Application Name:** SplitYourBill (Easy Split)  
**Package Name:** `com.easySplit.splityourbill`  

---

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **SplitYourBill** ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how our mobile application handles your information when you use our services.

**SplitYourBill is designed as an offline-first, privacy-focused application.** We believe your personal expense and financial sharing data should remain strictly under your control.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information
**We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal identifiable information (PII).**

Any information you enter into the application—including:
- Group names
- Participant / member names
- Transaction amounts and categories
- Split and settlement calculations

is stored **exclusively in a private local SQLite database on your device**.

### B. Device & Diagnostic Data
- **No Internet Access:** The application does **not** request or use the `android.permission.INTERNET` permission.
- **No Remote Transmission:** None of your data can leave your device through the application.
- **No Tracking:** We do not track your device identifier, IP address, MAC address, GPS location, or usage patterns.

---

## 3. Device Permissions

SplitYourBill requires **no runtime or sensitive Android device permissions**. Specifically, the app does not request access to:
- Internet or Network State
- Contacts or Phonebook
- Camera or Photos
- Device Storage / Media
- Location Services
- Microphone or Audio

---

## 4. Third-Party Services & Trackers

SplitYourBill does **not** integrate any third-party SDKs, services, or APIs that collect user data, such as:
- Advertising networks (e.g., AdMob, Unity Ads)
- Analytics services (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel)
- Crash-reporting SDKs that transmit telemetry off-device

---

## 5. Data Retention & Deletion

- **Local Storage:** All user-generated content resides solely in the app's local sandbox storage directory on your physical device.
- **In-App Reset:** You can delete all group data, member names, and transaction history at any time using the **Reset** button within the app.
- **App Data Removal:** Uninstalling the application or clearing the application storage via **Settings > Apps > SplitYourBill > Storage > Clear Data** immediately and permanently deletes all stored data.

---

## 6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Because SplitYourBill does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data from any user, it does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 16 in certain jurisdictions). If you believe that your child has entered information into the app on their device, this data remains solely on that device and can be cleared at any time.

---

## 7. Security

We take the security of your data seriously. By keeping all operations and data storage local to your device and foregoing network communication entirely, SplitYourBill eliminates exposure to cloud database leaks, network eavesdropping, or server-side breaches. We recommend maintaining a secure lock screen on your device to protect local data.

---

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect updates to our application or applicable legal requirements. Any modifications will be indicated by the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. Since the app has no network access to push policy changes, we encourage you to review the latest policy hosted on our official project repository or Google Play Store listing when updating the app.
